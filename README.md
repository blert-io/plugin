# blert

[Blert](https://blert.io) is an Old School RuneScape PvM data tracker and analysis system.

The plugin records your PvM encounters as you play and uploads them to
[blert.io](https://blert.io), where you can review every room tick-by-tick and
track your performance over time.

## Supported content

- Theatre of Blood
- Fortis Colosseum
- Inferno
- Doom of Mokhaiotl

## Getting started

1. Sign up for free at [blert.io/register](https://blert.io/register).
2. Open the [API Keys page](https://blert.io/settings/api-keys) in your Blert
   settings and create a key for your OSRS account.
3. Open the Blert side panel in RuneLite, paste your key into the field
   under "Getting Started", and press Connect.

Once connected, supported challenges are recorded automatically as you play. Your recordings
appear in the side panel and on the site.

For help, feedback, or progress updates, join our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/c5Hgv3NnYe).
